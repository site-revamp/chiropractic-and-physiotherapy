A more connected approach

One team. Different perspectives. Care centred on you.

When pain, stiffness or restricted movement gets in the way, finding the right support can feel like another challenge. At Hungerford Chiropractic & Physiotherapy Healthcare, our practitioners work together across different areas of care.

Whether you are looking for help with back or neck discomfort, recovering from an injury, managing muscular tension or hoping to move with greater confidence, we start by understanding your needs and what you want to achieve.

Our aim is to help you get better and stay well for longer, with care and recommendations that take your individual circumstances into account.

Your goals, your questions and your everyday life belong at the centre of your care.

Your first visit

Know what to expect before you arrive.

Starting with a new healthcare practitioner can feel unfamiliar. Here is a simple overview of the first steps at the clinic.

01

Tell us what is happening

You will be welcomed by the team and asked about your health history, your current concerns and how they affect everyday life.

02

Assessment and examination

Your practitioner will discuss your concerns and carry out an appropriate examination, which may include movement, posture and relevant physical tests.

03

Understand your options

Your practitioner will explain their findings, discuss suitable options and answer questions so you can make an informed decision.

Planning your first appointment: the clinic asks new patients to arrive 10 minutes early and allow around an hour for the initial appointment. Treatment is not provided at the first visit unless agreed with the practitioner in advance. Read the first-visit guide →
