Hungerford Chiropractic & Physiotherapy Healthcare Homepage Redesign
1. Website Overview
Hungerford Chiropractic & Physiotherapy Healthcare is a family-oriented healthcare practice based in Hungerford, Berkshire. Established in 2005, the clinic provides chiropractic care, physiotherapy, massage therapy and related treatments to individuals and families across Hungerford, Newbury and surrounding communities.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the clinic as an approachable, multidisciplinary healthcare provider. It makes the available services easier to discover, communicates the clinic's care offering more clearly and strengthens patient confidence through better presentation of its team and patient information.
2. Main Improvements
1. Clearer Homepage Messaging
- Reworked the homepage messaging to communicate the clinic's combined chiropractic, physiotherapy and massage services.
- Gave greater visibility to the clinic's range of healthcare treatments.
- Improved the presentation of its family-oriented approach to patient care.
Problem addressed: The previous homepage did not immediately communicate the full range of services or help visitors understand the available treatment options.
2. Improved Service Discovery and Navigation
- Organised key treatments into clearer service categories.
- Improved the visibility of chiropractic, physiotherapy and massage therapy.
- Created a more straightforward navigation experience for visitors exploring treatment options.
Problem addressed: Services were spread across different pages, requiring visitors to navigate around the website to find relevant treatments.
3. Stronger Trust and Credibility
- Improved the presentation of the clinic's experienced healthcare team.
- Gave greater prominence to patient testimonials and first-visit information.
- Structured trust-building content to support visitors considering an initial appointment.
Problem addressed: Important credibility information, including team experience, testimonials and first-visit guidance, was not sufficiently prominent on the previous homepage.
3. Design Improvements
- Visual Presentation: Applied the supplied primary brand colour, #158849, to support a professional, reassuring and health-focused appearance.
- Homepage Layout: Improved content hierarchy and section organisation to give services, team information and patient guidance clearer visibility.
- User Experience: Simplified service discovery and made treatment information easier to access.
- Content Consistency: Presented chiropractic, physiotherapy and massage services within a more cohesive homepage structure.
- Overall Appearance: Reinforced the clinic's approachable, family-oriented identity through a consistent visual direction and clearer content presentation.
